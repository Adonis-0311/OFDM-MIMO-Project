Verified requirements and package status:

- Double-column, single-spaced, 10-point IEEE format: satisfied by `IEEEtran` journal mode.
- Initial Regular Paper limit of 13 pages including references: main manuscript is 8 pages.
- Abstract length of 150--250 words and self-contained: 212 source-counted words; no references, footnotes, or displayed equations.
- Main manuscript contains the complete problem, method, theory, experimental protocol, primary comparisons, and conclusions. It contains no reference to supplementary material.
- Supplementary material is optional expanded evidence for specialist inspection, is uploaded separately, and is 6 pages.
- Author names and affiliations are present; portal author order and affiliations must match the manuscript.
- ORCID is required for every author in the Author Portal.
- EDICS selection is required during submission; suggested current categories are listed in `submission_metadata.md`.
- AI-assisted tools are disclosed only as language-polishing support. Technical content, results, figures, and conclusions remain the authors' responsibility.
- A prior scope-only prescreen rejection is disclosed in a separate supporting document.
- Figures are vector PDF in the manuscript source package; color encodings also use markers, labels, or luminance contrast.
