{\Large\bfseries Prior Scope-Rejection Disclosure for IEEE TSP}
\end{center}

The manuscript was previously submitted to \emph{IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems} as TAES-2026-3012 and was rejected during prescreening on 27 August 2026 for journal-scope reasons. It did not undergo external peer review, and no technical review reports were issued.

The prescreening decision stated that the work centers on sparse-estimation algorithms and complexity analysis rather than the design or integration of an aerospace system or subsystem. The present submission has therefore been prepared for \emph{IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing} around its signal-processing contribution: FFT-selected tensor neighborhoods, multidimensional Candan refinement, fitted-subspace projection selection, probabilistic error propagation, and matched reconstruction and parameter-accuracy evaluation.

This framing falls within the TSP scope covering signal estimation, sparse and tensor signal processing, performance analysis, and radar/communication signal applications. The previous decision was based solely on the mismatch between those contributions and the aerospace-system scope of the former venue.
