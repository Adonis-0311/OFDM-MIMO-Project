# 2. Related Work

## Sparse and Tensor-Based Estimation in ISAC

High-frequency communication and sensing channels are often represented by a small number of dominant propagation or target components, which makes sparse recovery a natural baseline for mmWave MIMO-OFDM estimation. Matching pursuit and orthogonal matching pursuit established greedy sparse approximation as an interpretable alternative to dense inversion (`mallat1993matching`, `pati1993omp`, `tropp2007omp`), while multiple-measurement-vector sparse recovery formalized the value of shared support across related observations (`cotter2005mmv`). These classical references explain why a Tensor-OMP scaffold is a natural starting point, but they do not by themselves address off-grid physical parameters or trainable target supervision.

Tensor formulations add another important prior: angle, delay, Doppler, antenna, subcarrier, and snapshot dimensions can be kept separable instead of being collapsed into a single vectorized dictionary. Tensor factorization and uniqueness results provide the broader mathematical background for such separable representations (`sidiropoulos2000parallel`, `sidiropoulos2000unique`, `kolda2009tensor`). A close ISAC reference point is the unified massive-MIMO tensor formulation of `zhang2024unified_tensor_isac`, which models channel and target-parameter estimation through tensor-decomposition structures. T-OMP-Net builds on this established tensor-sparse view rather than replacing it. Its role is to turn a Tensor-OMP recovery scaffold into a trainable, bounded off-grid estimator for the FDMA virtual-array setting.

Sparse mmWave channel-estimation and hybrid-precoding work provides a second classical neighborhood. Channel-estimation and sparse-precoding formulations for millimeter-wave MIMO (`alkhateeb2014channel`, `ayach2014spatially`) and broader mmWave signal-processing surveys (`heath2016overview`, `rangan2014mmwave`) motivate physically structured channel descriptions. The present manuscript adds held-out CDL-A/C evaluation while keeping unseen CDL-D as a failure boundary.

## Deep Unfolding for Wireless and JCAS

Deep unfolding converts iterative optimization or sparse-recovery procedures into trainable networks whose layers retain an algorithmic interpretation. This design principle is now well established in wireless systems, as summarized in `deka2025deep_unfolding_review`. In joint communication and sensing, `nguyen2024jcas_deep_unfolding_hbf` unfolds a hybrid-beamforming optimizer to manage the communication-sensing trade-off. That work is close in philosophy because both approaches preserve a model-driven iterative structure, but the optimization object is different: hybrid beamforming designs transmit and receive beams, whereas T-OMP-Net estimates sparse channel/target parameters over an angle-delay-Doppler tensor.

## Off-Grid Sparse Recovery and Model-Driven Refinement

Discretized sparse estimators are sensitive to grid mismatch: a target or path that lies between grid atoms can be represented by a biased or spread support estimate. Off-grid sparse Bayesian inference, represented by `yang2013ogsbi`, is an important ancestor because it treats the offset between physical parameters and dictionary atoms as part of the inference problem. The refinement used in T-OMP-Net is deliberately more local and constrained. After support selection, angle, delay, and Doppler offsets are refined within bounded neighborhoods around selected atoms, so the learned correction remains tied to the physical dictionary. The resulting claim is therefore a support-conditioned local off-grid improvement over grid Tensor-OMP, not a claim of globally optimal continuous sparse recovery.

## Learning Objectives for ISAC Target Estimation

Learning-based ISAC estimators must balance communication-channel reconstruction with sensing-oriented target accuracy. OFDM radar-communication fusion and modern ISAC surveys establish the dual-functional signal-processing context (`sturm2011waveform`, `liu2022isac`), and OFDM/OTFS comparisons show that waveform choices can materially affect joint radar-communication performance (`gaudio2019ofdm_otfs`). Broader AI-empowered ISAC surveys such as `vaezi2025ai_empowered_isac_tutorial` place model-driven learning in the wider context of communication-sensing co-design. A closer channel-estimation neighbor is `yang2026radar_cenet`, which uses radar-derived angle information inside a deep-unfolded sparse channel-estimation network.

T-OMP-Net differs in its FDMA virtual-array tensor setting and in its explicit multi-target parameter objective. Because the same target set can be listed in different orders, the training loss uses assignment-based matching to make the target-parameter term permutation-invariant. This use is algorithmic rather than a novelty claim: the Hungarian method and later assignment formulations are classical optimization tools (`kuhn1955hungarian`, `munkres1957assignment`). The manuscript also treats CRLB and impairment sensitivity as bounded analysis surfaces: standard estimation theory anchors the high-SNR CRLB check (`kay1993fundamentals`), while non-ideal massive-MIMO hardware analysis motivates keeping hardware impairment robustness as a future-work boundary rather than an established claim (`bjornson2014nonideal`).

## External Benchmark Context

DeepMIMO provides a widely used ray-tracing benchmark context for mmWave and massive-MIMO research. The original framework is described in `alkhateeb2019deepmimo`, while `deepmimo2026toolchain` documents the active toolchain. The current DeepMIMO runs remain scalar-transfer proxies, so the trained physical external claim is carried by CDL rather than by ray tracing.

## Closest-Work Boundary

The closest related threads are therefore complementary rather than redundant. `zhang2024unified_tensor_isac` establishes the relevance of tensor models for ISAC parameter estimation; T-OMP-Net adds a trainable Tensor-OMP path with bounded physical off-grid refinement. `nguyen2024jcas_deep_unfolding_hbf` demonstrates deep unfolding for JCAS beamforming; T-OMP-Net applies unfolding to sparse tensor recovery and target estimation. `yang2026radar_cenet` shows sensing-assisted unfolded channel estimation; T-OMP-Net instead couples FDMA virtual-array sparse recovery with permutation-invariant multi-target supervision. The contribution is the evidence-backed combination of these ingredients for bounded off-grid mmWave MIMO-OFDM ISAC estimation, together with a restricted high-load early-stop result, rather than novelty in any single ingredient alone.
