## Current Coverage Verdict

The evidence package supports a one-parameter local mechanism and a separate 210-parameter controller on held-out CDL-A/C. It does not support a unified deep K-stage T-OMP-Net; A4 is a deterministic depth diagnostic. E8e prevents a profile-universal claim because zero-shot CDL-D degrades channel and delay, and E2b--E2d prevent a broad SNR-axis stopping claim. The package is not submission-complete because matched-accuracy continuous-recovery comparators, larger G2/A4 statistics, trained DeepMIMO physical evaluation, and a unified estimator remain open.
